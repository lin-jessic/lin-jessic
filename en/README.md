## 01. PineNose

**Non-destructive Pineapple Ripeness and Variety Detection System**  
*Graduation Project at Chang Gung University · Team Project*

PineNose combines gas sensors, machine learning, computer vision, and edge computing to estimate pineapple ripeness without cutting the fruit. Arduino collects sensor readings, Raspberry Pi runs ripeness inference, and YOLOv8 with EfficientNet-B0 supports image-based variety identification. Results are presented through web and mobile interfaces for farmers and consumers.

As team leader, I worked on system integration, database and Docker workflows, connecting sensor and image-recognition components, testing, and troubleshooting.

**Technologies:** Python, Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ExtraTrees, YOLOv8, EfficientNet-B0, Flask, Docker

**Recognition:** Gold Award, 2026 Agricultural Innovation Technology Competition

## 02. FreqFuseNet

**3D Head-and-Neck Organ-at-Risk Segmentation**  
*Medical Image Segmentation · Research*

FreqFuseNet investigates feature-scale differences between FFT and FcaNet frequency branches in CT segmentation of thin-wall head-and-neck organs at risk. The method explores scale normalization and residual fusion for more consistent feature integration.

I contributed to model experiments, ablation studies, baseline comparisons, result analysis, and manuscript-related work. The research uses SegRap2023 and has a publicly available medRxiv preprint.

**Technologies:** PyTorch, 3D CT, Deep Learning, Frequency-domain Features, SegRap2023

[Research Repository](https://github.com/Tiffanyxxx3238/freq-spatial-headneck-seg)　 ·　 [medRxiv Preprint](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.09.26357642v2)
